# IEEE Access Submission Checklist

## Completed in this workspace

- Official IEEE Access class and support files, template revision May 13, 2026.
- Research-article structure and one-paragraph 150--250-word abstract.
- Funding statement in `\tfootnote`.
- Author block, common affiliation, and five no-photo biographies.
- AI-assistance disclosure in the Acknowledgment.
- Verified bibliography and DOI metadata.
- Source-backed table/figure generation and validation framework.
- Explicit code/data availability and experimental-scope statements.
- Finalized method-workflow figure and reproducible vector-figure sources.

## Scientific and measurement gates before submission

- Complete and validate the repaired-ComNetX campaign under
  `results/ieee-access-2026-1/raw/repaired-comnetx/`; do not reuse historical
  multilevel smart-mode values as repaired results.
- Keep LD-Leiden as a cited methodological comparison, not a quantitative row,
  unless an independently executable artifact and a genuinely matched
  initialization/timing protocol become available. Do not compute a speedup
  from the completed standalone diagnostic campaign.
- Require zero hierarchy, scope-collision, cache-nesting, and outside-write
  failures for the closure-enabled method. Validate the radius-only control by
  outside partition equivalence rather than numeric label equality.
- Report the boundary-certificate quantities and establish whether the bound
  is informative on the measured streams before promoting it as a headline
  result.
- Regenerate every numeric macro, table, and figure from the accepted campaign
  records, then re-audit all prose values against the generated artifacts.
- Preserve all 67 verified conference references and verify every additional
  journal reference; remove none merely to shorten the article.
- Complete the five-RQ results structure: effect, backend generality,
  localization mechanism, component controls, and operating envelope.
- Run the final source/PDF consistency, citation, font, file-size, and rendered
  page checks only after measurements and manuscript text are frozen.

## Upload-time checks

- Upload `main.tex`, `references.bib`, the IEEE Access support files, and all
  files in `figures/` and `generated/`; upload the matching PDF.
- Keep the complete source archive and PDF below 40 MB.
- Select Research Article and enter 3--10 submission keywords.
- Paste the funding, corresponding-author, data/code-availability, and
  AI-assistance disclosures into the matching submission fields.
- Review the generated proof; do not add a graphical abstract unless the
  journal requests one and it undergoes review.
