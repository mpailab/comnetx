# IEEE Access Submission Checklist

## Completed in this workspace

- Official IEEE Access class and support files, template revision May 13, 2026.
- Research-article structure and one-paragraph 150--250-word abstract.
- Funding statement in `\tfootnote`.
- Author block, common affiliation, and five no-photo biographies.
- AI-assistance disclosure in the Acknowledgment.
- Verified bibliography and DOI metadata.
- Source-backed tables and vector figures generated by
  `analysis/validate_results.py`.
- Explicit code/data availability and limitations statements.
- Final source/PDF consistency, reference, font, size, and rendered-page checks.

## Author confirmation required before upload

- Designate the corresponding author. If the authors select Grigoriy Bokov,
  confirm that `bokovgrigoriy@gmail.com` is the desired submission e-mail, then
  add the corresponding-author line to `main.tex`.
- Approve the author order, spelling, biographies, and the common postal code
  `119991`.
- Supply or confirm every author's ORCID in the submission system. Publicly
  matched identifiers should not be inserted without the author's approval.
- Confirm whether any author needs a second Faculty of Mechanics and
  Mathematics affiliation.
- Confirm the funding wording and identifiers
  `000000C313925P4H0002` and `139-15-2025-012`.
- Confirm that the public repository and archived measurement bundle may be
  exposed to reviewers at submission time.
- Publish the journal source and measurement bundle as a versioned release or
  archival snapshot, then replace the moving branch link in `main.tex` with
  that stable URL. Do not add a repository DOI unless one has been minted.
- Declare the related preprint arXiv:2608.16906 in the submission form or cover
  letter as required by the journal.

## Upload-time checks

- Upload `main.tex`, `references.bib`, the IEEE Access support files, and all
  files in `figures/` and `generated/`; upload the matching PDF.
- Keep the complete source archive and PDF below 40 MB.
- Select Research Article and enter 3--10 submission keywords.
- Paste the funding, corresponding-author, data/code-availability, and
  AI-assistance disclosures into the matching submission fields.
- Review the generated proof; do not add a graphical abstract unless the
  journal requests one and it undergoes review.
